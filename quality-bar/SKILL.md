# Studio Quality Bar

Review this list **before showing anything**. If a build fails it, fix the issue first and do not present it.

## General rules

1. **Nothing that looks like a PDF or a document.** Panels and dashboards are real interactive products with animation, not blocks of text or tables.
2. **Commercial game quality.** Art follows a mixed system: concept art with AI is for design only and must be approved; then characters and objects are real 3D, rigged and animated; then the field, light, shadows, particles and UI are built in code. Everything needs volume, light and shadows, and is created for that project. Forbidden: random web images, stock packs, assets from other projects, flat images pasted into the game, flat boards, and characters made of crude blocks.
3. **Really animated.** Characters walk with a step cycle, they move, and there are particles and secondary motion. Nothing is a still sticker.
4. **Characters are not deformed.** Uniform scale and believable proportion. Never stretch a sprite to fill a gap.
5. **Optimised for mobile.** Portrait unless the design says otherwise, touch first, large buttons (48 px minimum, 44 px absolute floor), and a fast load.
6. **Real data only.** Anything without a source is labelled "No data yet". Never invent numbers or quotes.
7. **Faithful to the approved reference.** If a reference image was approved, the result should clearly match it in layout, markers and style — without pasting the reference into the game.
8. **Ship the product in English** unless the brief says otherwise.
9. **Inspect the screenshot yourself** before sharing it, and compare it with the reference and with these rules.

## Game rules

10. **Simple, intuitive and addictive.** One mechanic that is clear on the first tap, with no wall of text. If a prototype is not immediately understandable, rebuild around a clearer loop.
11. **Simple is not ugly.** Visual quality stays high: animation, juice, a striking menu, and covers that work at a glance.
12. **Vary the genre.** Each new game should be a different genre from the last one, not the same puzzle in a new skin.
13. **Learn from the best, do not copy.** Study top games in the genre and beat them on at least 3 concrete points. Copy technique, never code or art.
14. **A studio signature.** A mascot, a short intro if it does not delay play, a shared button and results kit, and a victory flourish used across titles.
15. **Nothing is ready for review without the delivery set.** Zip, covers, store listing art, screenshots, a GIF or short clip, and written audits.

## Screen rules

16. **Nothing floating or empty.** Every label sits in a designed container, there are no blank artless regions, and no text is clipped or overlapped.
17. **Real covers always.** Never a placeholder.
18. **A quality card per game** with screenshots, notes, a step-by-step review and a history of rounds.

## How to use this skill

- Before delivery: walk the rules one by one against the final screenshot.
- After a correction: add a short, concrete rule here so the failure is not repeated.

## Extra lessons

19. **Realistic video-game style in every title.** No flat clip-art. Detailed characters with volume and live animation, textures, real light and shadow, and no careless visual errors. Compare against the best mobile games in the same genre, not against an earlier in-house prototype.
20. **Filter before review.** Do not send a game for review without a short note of the skills used, a visual filter (compared with top mobile games in the genre) and a gameplay filter. If any screen falls below the bar, return it for another pass.
21. **Premium art comes first.** The main play screen (board or pitch) is designed and approved at commercial quality **before** gameplay code. Never present schematic placeholder art as a progress shot.
22. **Logic must be visible on screen.** Each side has its own constant colour, board lines use a third neutral colour, and every line that matters to play is actually drawn.
23. **The bar is "premium commercial".** Light, shadows, texture, depth and animation on everything.
